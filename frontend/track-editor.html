💡 Quick Guide

  • Outer Boundary: Click points to create track walls (min 3 points)
  • Inner Boundary: Create inner walls for closed circuits
  • Spawn Point: Click to set car start position, drag arrow for direction
  • Finish Line: Click two points to create race finish
  • Move Points: Drag existing points to reposition
  • Delete Point: Right-click or press Delete key
  • Pan: Middle-click and drag
  • Zoom: Mouse wheel
